Google Ads · Australia

Google Ads Search Campaign

Website reservation-focused Google Ads campaign with strategic conversion tracking to optimize for real business outcomes.

Challenge: Multiple conversions tracked equally made it hard to evaluate true booking performance.

Strategy: Separated website reservations as primary conversion, reclassified calls/directions as secondary.

Key insight: Tracking multiple actions is valuable — but separating primary vs secondary conversions is critical for accurate optimization.

8.07%CTR

Google Maps · Australia

Google Maps & Local Intent Strategy

The restaurant wanted to generate consistent local demand through Google Ads. Most customers were searching on mobile, often close to dining time, and making quick decisions based on location, directions, and availability.

Challenge: Broad match keywords triggered irrelevant searches, high CPC at A$2.45, and inconsistent traffic quality.

Strategy: Shifted from broad match to exact match keywords and pivoted focus to Google Maps visibility.

Key insight: Understanding how customers search is more important than pushing traffic. Google Maps is often the highest-intent channel.

455Direction requests
A$0.41Avg CPC

Facebook & Instagram · Australia

Organic Social Media Growth System

The restaurant needed stronger online visibility but had a limited advertising budget. Growth had to come from content consistency, discoverability, and community engagement, not paid reach.

Challenge: A limited advertising budget meant visibility had to be earned through consistent content, discoverability, and community engagement.

Strategy: Built repeatable content framework focused on discovery formats (Reels, Stories, hashtags).

Key insight: Organic growth becomes predictable when content is treated as a system, not random posting.

Local SEO · Australia

Local SEO & GBP Transformation

The business struggled with low local visibility despite strong service quality. Rankings were mostly outside page one, limiting inbound inquiries.

Challenge: Rankings between #14–#41 with weak map-pack presence and under-optimized Google Business Profile.

Strategy: Focused on local relevance signals with GBP as primary asset, including geotagged images and citation building.

Key insight: For local businesses, Google Business Profile optimization is often the highest-leverage SEO activity.

#1Final ranking
#21-41Previous ranking

Meta Ads · Australia

Meta Ads Performance System for Local Restaurant Growth

The restaurant wanted to increase local visibility, engagement, and customer inquiries using Meta Ads. The challenge was understanding which campaign objectives actually worked for a local restaurant environment.

Challenge: Unclear performance between traffic, engagement, and messaging campaigns with risk of wasted spend.

Strategy: Implemented a structured testing framework with one clear objective per campaign and separate audience tests.

Key insight: Meta Ads performance depends on objective clarity and structured testing — not budget size.
